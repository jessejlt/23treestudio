# Shopify brand copy — shop.23treestudio.com

Paste-ready consumer copy drawn from the 23 Tree Studio brand story. Written for the
Shopify storefront only. It is deliberately different from the buyer-focused Faire brand
story, and Faire content sync stays off.

The long-form story lives on the main site at <https://23treestudio.com/our-story.html>.

---

## 1. Homepage section — "Our Story" (2–3 sentences)

**Label (small caps):** Our Story

**Body:**

> 23 Tree Studio began with tiny Colorado landscapes, painted on saved scraps of canvas and
> finished with 23 karat gold. The gold never left: it runs through our paint sets, brushes,
> and the Juliette, all designed by a one-person studio in Castle Rock, Colorado. Beautiful
> supplies, worth reaching for.

**Button:** Read our story → `https://23treestudio.com/our-story.html`

**Where to place it:** Online Store → Themes → Customize → Home page → *Add section* →
**Rich text** (or **Image with text** once a brand photo exists). Put the label in the
section's caption/eyebrow field, the body in the text field, and the button in the button
field. Position it directly below the hero or above the footer.

## 2. Footer blurb (2 sentences)

> Watercolor paint sets, brushes, and the Juliette clutch, designed by a one-person studio
> in Castle Rock, Colorado. It started with tiny gold-leaf landscapes, and the gold stayed.

**Where to place it:** Customize → Footer → *Add block* → **Text**. Heading "23 Tree Studio"
(or leave blank if the logo already sits in the footer).

Use either the homepage section or the footer blurb, or both. They are written to stand
alone and not to repeat each other.

## 3. Juliette product page — one story line

Add as the first short paragraph of the description on
`/products/the-juliette-watercolor-wristlet`:

> The Juliette is named for my daughter, who loves to dress up, and it came from wanting
> beautiful supplies to travel alongside the watercolor workbooks I stock in the shop.

**Where to place it:** Products → The Juliette Watercolor Wristlet → Description. Keep the
existing feature copy (padded book-sleeve construction, pockets, zip-around closure, wrist
strap, approx. 12 × 8 in.) below it.

## Brand standards in the Shopify theme editor

Set once under Customize → **Theme settings**:

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Accent / buttons | Gold `#D09F4F` |
| Text | Navy `#162C45` |
| Background | Cream `#F6F1E7` |
| Heading font | Cormorant Garamond (Shopify's font library includes it; choose the italic semibold cut where the theme allows per-element styles) |
| Label / caption font | Montserrat Bold, letter-spacing 0.19em where the theme exposes it |
| Logo | Upload an unmodified export of `23TreeStudio_Logo_Gold_VECTOR.pdf` (SVG or PNG) under Header → Logo. Never re-typeset the 23|T mark. |

## Guardrails checklist (retail storefront)

- [ ] No **compare-at price** on any product, so no "Sale" badge renders for retail customers.
- [ ] Wholesale pricing stays in B2B catalogs / price lists assigned to company locations only, never on the retail storefront.
- [ ] No phone number in the footer, contact page, or policies.
- [ ] Do not name the watercolor workbook brand stocked in the shop; use "watercolor workbooks".
- [ ] Do not name any manufacturer in product copy.
- [ ] Faire content sync remains off.
- [ ] Never compare the Juliette to a cosmetic bag, repurposed or otherwise; that framing was never what it is.
